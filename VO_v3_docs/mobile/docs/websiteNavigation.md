# Website Navigation

The **[VachanOnline](https://beta.vachanonline.com/)** landing page provides quick access to Bible reading, audio, videos, and other Scripture resources. From the landing page, users can select their preferred language, start reading or listening to the Bible, and explore available resources. 

## **Landing Page**

**Header** 

The header is displayed at the top of the landing page and provides access to the VachanOnline logo, search, and user options. 

<img src="/img/assets/version3/homepage.png"  width="400px" alt="homepage" className="img-border"/>

## **VachanOnline Logo** 

Click the **VachanOnline logo (1)** from anywhere on the website to return directly to the landing page. This provides an easy way to navigate back to the main page without using the browser's back button or navigating through the menu. 

## **Search** 

A user can quickly look up specific chapters, verses, passages, paragraphs, or keywords directly from the landing page by clicking the **Search icon (2)**. The search feature helps users find relevant Bible content without having to manually navigate through books and chapters. 

## **Profile** 

Click the **Sign In/Avatar icon (3)** and select Profile to access your profile. The Profile page provides an overview of your activity and saved content in Vachan Online. It includes Highlights, Bookmarks, Notes, and History, allowing you to easily access highlighted verses, saved content, personal notes, and recent reading activity. 

## **Hero Section**

The hero section introduces VachanOnline with the message: 

Read, Listen, Watch and Experience God's Word in your Heart language 

It provides quick access to Bible reading and audio. 

<img src="/img/assets/version3/homepage1.png"  width="400px" alt="homepage1" className="img-border"/>

## **Start Reading** 

Click **Start Reading (4)** to open the Bible reading experience. This takes you directly to the reading interface, where you can begin reading the Bible and access the available reading features. From the reading screen, you can navigate between books and chapters, select specific verses, search for passages or keywords, and use other available options such as Parallel bibles, audio, videos and commentary. 

## **Listen Audio** 

Click **Listen Audio (5)** to access the Audio Bible. This opens the audio reading experience, where you can listen to the Bible being narrated instead of reading the text. You can select the required book and chapter and use the available playback controls to play, pause, resume, or navigate through the audio. 

## **Today's Verse** 

The Today's **Verse card (6)** displays a selected Bible verse for the day. 

The card includes: 

- Bible verse 

- Bible reference 

- Date 

- Read Now option 

Click **Read Now** to open the displayed verse in the Read Bible page. From there, you can read the entire chapter and navigate to other books and chapters of the Bible.

## **Easily Access Features with a Click**

As you scroll through the landing page, you can quickly access key features and resources through various cards. Each card provides a direct way to explore Bible content based on your preference. 

<img src="/img/assets/version3/homepage2.png"  width="400px" alt="homepage2" className="img-border"/>

- **Read Bible** 

- **Audio Bible** 

- **Videos** 

- **Sign Language Bible** 

- **Compare** 

- **Bible Stories** 

Click **View All** to display additional Bible resources. Click **View Less** to collapse the list and display fewer resource cards.

## **Popular Languages** 

The Popular Languages section displays languages in which Scripture is available. 

**Popular languages (1)** displayed on the landing page include: 

Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Gujarati, Marathi

## **Search for a Language** 

Use the **Search (2)** for a language field to quickly find a specific language. 

- Select a Language 

- Click a language card to access the available Scripture resources in that language. 

Click **View All (3)** to see and access the Bibles in Indian languages. 

## **Start Your Scripture Journey Today** 

The Start Your Scripture Journey Today section provides quick access to Bible reading and reading plans. 

- **Read the Bible (1)**

- **View Reading Plans (2)**

## **Download Vachan Malayalam** 

Click **Google Play (1)** to open the Vachan Malayalam application on the Google Play Store. From there, you can view the app details and tap Install to download and install the application on your device.

## **Footer** 

The footer is displayed at the bottom of the landing page and provides access to additional information and website options. 

The footer includes: 

- **About Us (2)** -Click About Us to learn more about VachanOnline.

- **What's New (3)** - Click What's New to view the latest updates and new features. 

- **Feedback (4)** - Click Feedback to provide feedback about your experience with VachanOnline. 

- **VachanOnline Classic (5)** - Tap Vachan Online Classic to navigate to the older version of the Vachan Online website. This option allows users to access the classic website and continue using its existing interface and features.
