# Audio Bible

To access Audio Bibles, click the **Listen Audio (1)** button or select the **Audio card** on the landing page.

<img src="/img/assets/version3/audiobible.png"  width="400px" alt="audiobible" className="img-border"/>

- The user can select the desired language from the **Language dropdown (2)** and choose the required book and chapter from the **Book dropdown (3)**. 

<img src="/img/assets/version3/audiobible1.png"  width="400px" alt="audiobible1" className="img-border"/>

- Users can adjust the **Playback Speed (7)** from 0.5× to 2×. 

- The **Sleep Timer (5)** can be set to 5 minutes, 10 minutes, 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour, or turned Off. 

- Click the **Play (6)** button to start audio playback. 

- The **Featured Audio Books (4)** section displays available books with their corresponding language and number of chapters.

- Click **Study Tools (8)** to access additional resources that support your Bible study.
