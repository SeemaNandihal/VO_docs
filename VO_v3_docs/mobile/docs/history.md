# History

The **History** page lets you see everything you've recently read on VachanOnline, so you can quickly jump back to a passage you were studying earlier.

**How to Access History**

- Open the **Read Bible page** for any book/chapter.
- Click **More Tools (1)** (next to Commentary, Parallel Bible, Audio, and ISLV).
- Select **History (2)** from the dropdown.

The History panel opens on the right side of your reading view.

<img src="/img/assets/version3/history.png"  width="400px" alt="history" className="img-border"/>


**What You'll See**

Your **reading history (3)** is automatically logged and grouped into time-based sections:

- **Today** — passages viewed in the current day, with a count (e.g. "Today (22)")
- **Yesterday** — passages viewed the previous day
- **This Week** — everything viewed so far this week

<img src="/img/assets/version3/history1.png"  width="400px" alt="history" className="img-border"/>

**Each entry in the list shows (3):**

- **Version** (e.g. Eng ESV)
- **Book & Chapter** (e.g. Proverbs 3, Revelation 12)
- **Time since viewed** (e.g. "5 min ago", "1 day ago")
- A **trash/delete** icon to remove that individual entry

**Searching Your History (4)**

<img src="/img/assets/version3/history2.png"  width="400px" alt="history" className="img-border"/>

Use the **Search (4)** by book or version field at the top of the panel to quickly filter your history and find a specific book or translation you looked up earlier.

Use the **sort dropdown (5)** (default Most recent) to reorder your history by **Most recent, Oldest first, or Book order**.

**Refresh, Clear History, and Show More**

- Click **Refresh (6)** to reload the list with your latest activity.
- Click **Delete (6)** to permanently delete your entire reading
