# Audio Bible

The Oral Bibles page is designed for languages that do not have a written script, allowing users to listen to Scripture through audio. 

To access Oral Bibles, click the **Listen Audio** button or select the **Audio card** on the landing page.

- The user can select the desired language from the **Language dropdown (1)** and choose the required book and chapter from the **Book dropdown (2)**. 

- Users can adjust the **Playback Speed (4)** from 0.5× to 2×. 

- The **Sleep Timer (5)** can be set to 5 minutes, 10 minutes, 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour, or turned Off. 

- Click the **Play (6)** button to start audio playback. 

- The **Featured Audio Books (3)** section displays available books with their corresponding language and number of chapters.

- Click **Study Tools (7)** to access additional resources that support your Bible study.
