# commentary

- To access Commentaries, click **Study Tools** on the Read Bible page and select the **Commentary (1)**.

- Select the Bible in your preferred **language and version (2)**, then choose the required **book and chapter (3)** from the drop-down menu.

- The preferred commentaries can be chosen from the Commentary drop-down menu **(4)**.

- The introductory sections of selected commentary books can be found under the introduction drop-down **(5)**.
