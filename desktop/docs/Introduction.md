# Introduction

**VachanOnline** is a Scripture engagement platform designed to make the Bible accessible and meaningful to users across Indian languages. 

The platform serves a diverse range of users: 

**Everyday Readers, Bible Believers, and Jesus Seekers** can freely access, read, and meditate on God's Word in the language of their choice. 

**Bible Students, Scholars, and Theologians** can explore Scripture in greater depth using features such as verse highlighting, notes, Bible version comparison, and topic-based study. 

**Church Elders and Pastors** can use study resources such as notes, commentaries,  and dictionaries to prepare sermons, deepen their understanding of Scripture, and support personal Bible study. 

**Youth and Children** can engage with Scripture through more than just Bible text. Audio, videos,and Bible stories provide visual and interactive ways to understand and explore God's Word. 

The platform includes the following features: 

1. [Read Bible](./readBible) – Read the Bible in your preferred language and access available Bible versions. 

2. [Audio Bible](./audioBible) – Listen to Scripture in your preferred language. 

3. [Commentary](./commentary) – Study Scripture using commentaries and additional explanations. 

4. [Sign Language Bible](./signLanguageBible) – Access Bible content in sign language for the Deaf community. 

**More tools**

5. [Dictionary](./dictionary) – Look up biblical words, terms, and their meanings. 

6. [Videos](./videos) – Watch Bible-related videos and other Scripture-based content. 

7. [Search](./searchBible) – Search for specific verses, words, or passages across the Bible. 

8. [Reading Plan](./readingPlan) – Follow structured reading plans to build a consistent Bible-reading habit. 

9. [Bible Stories](./bibleStories) – Discover Bible stories presented in an easy-to-understand format.

10. [Compare](./compare) – Compare different Bible versions side by side to gain a deeper understanding of Scripture. 

11. [History](./history) - The History feature keeps track of the Bible passages you have recently viewed, making it easy to return to previously read chapters without searching again.
