# Sign Language Bible

<img src="/img/assets/Versionv3/islv.png" width="1000px" alt="islv" />

- It is a simple-to-use tool that can assist the deaf community in knowing Jesus and living a life that matters.

- The user can access the Sign Language Bible from the **Landing Page** or from the Read Bible page by opening **Study Tools** and selecting the **ISLV** tab.

- To access ISLV, open the Read Bible page, click Study Tools, and select the **ISLV (1)** tab from the tools panel. 

- On the left Bible Reading Page, the user can select the **language and version (2)**, **book and chapter (3)** of the Bible.

- The videos for the selected chapter appear on the right pane.

- The user can click on the video thumbnail to play the Sign Bible video.

- Video description is accessible by clicking the downward pointing arrow **(4)**.

- Use the video controls to play, pause, adjust the playback position, change the volume, download, or view the video in full-screen mode.
