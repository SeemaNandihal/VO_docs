# Sign Language Bible

- It is a simple-to-use tool that can assist the deaf community in knowing Jesus and living a life that matters.

- The user can access the **Sign Language Bible** from the **Landing Page**.  
<img src="/img/assets/version3/islvbible1.png"  width="400px" alt="islvbible" className="img-border"/>

- **Sign Language Bible** can also be accessed from the the Read Bible page by opening **Study Tools** and selecting the **ISLV** tab.

<img src="/img/assets/version3/studytool_islvbible.png"  width="400px" alt="islvbible1" className="img-border"/>

- To access ISLV, open the Read Bible page, click Study Tools, and select the **ISLV (2)** tab from the tools panel. 

- On the left Bible Reading Page, the user can select the **language and version (3)**, **book and chapter (4)** of the Bible.

- The videos for the selected chapter appear on the bottom pane.

- The user can click on the video thumbnail to play the Sign Bible video.

- Video description **(6)** is accessible by clicking the downward pointing arrow.

- Use the right **(7)** and left **(8)** navigation arrows on either side of the reading area to move between chapters.

- Use the video controls to play, pause, adjust the playback position, change the volume, download, or view the video in full-screen mode.
