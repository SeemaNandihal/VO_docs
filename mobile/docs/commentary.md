# Commentary

<img src="/img/assets/version3/studytools.png"  width="400px" alt="studytools" className="img-border"/>

- To access Commentaries, click **Study Tools (1)** on the Read Bible page and select the **Commentary**.

- Select the Bible in your preferred **language and version (3)**, then choose the required **book and chapter (4)** from the drop-down menu.

- The preferred commentaries can be chosen from the Commentary drop-down menu **(5)**.

- The introductory sections of selected commentary books can be found under the introduction drop-down **(6)**.
