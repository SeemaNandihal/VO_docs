# Read Bible

The Bible Reading Page provides a focused reading experience where users can select a Bible version, preferred languages, Bible books and access additional reading and study tools. 

**Accessing the Bible Reading Page** 

The Bible Reading Page opens when you select **Start Reading** from the VachanOnline landing page.

<img src="/img/assets/version3/readbible.png"  width="400px" alt="readbible" className="img-border"/>

## Selecting a Language and Book

<img src="/img/assets/version3/readbible1.png"  width="400px" alt="readbible1" className="img-border"/>

To change the Bible translation: 

- Select your preferred language and Version **(1)**. 

- The **Book selector (2)** allows you to quickly navigate to a specific Bible book and chapter.

To open a specific chapter:
- Click the Book selector.
- Search for or select the required Bible book.
- Select the required chapter.
- The selected chapter opens in the reading area.

You can scroll through the page to continue reading the chapter and access additional verses. 

Use the **right (8)** and **left (9)** navigation arrows on either side of the reading area to move between chapters. 

## Sharing Bible Content 

The user must click the **share icon (1)**, copy the URL by choosing **copy to clipboard (2)**, and then share the bible chapter, passage, or verse on other platforms such as Facebook, Whats App, and so on.

<img src="/img/assets/version3/readbible2.png"  width="400px" alt="readbible2" className="img-border"/>

## My Library

The **My Library feature (4)**, accessible through the books icon, allows you to personalize your Bible reading experience by saving and organizing content for future reference. 

<img src="/img/assets/version3/readbible3.png"  width="400px" alt="readbible3" className="img-border"/>

**My Library includes:** 

**Bookmarks**: Save important chapters or verses so you can quickly return to them later.  

**Highlights**: Highlight verses or passages that you want to remember, reflect on, or refer to again.  

**Notes**: Add personal notes to Bible passages for reflection, study, or future reference.  

These personalized items are stored in My Library, allowing you to easily access your saved bookmarks, highlights, and notes while reading the Bible. 

**To access:**

Click the **My Library icon (1)** and select the feature you want to access. 

## Settings 

<img src="/img/assets/version3/readbible4.png"  width="400px" alt="readbible4" className="img-border"/>


Users can customize their reading experience by clicking the **Settings icon (1)** to choose a font, adjust the font size, enable Line View, and turn the Hover Verse feature on or off to keep focus on the verse being read. When studying the Bible, users can also enable or disable Cross-References and Footnotes according to their preference. Click the Information icon to view details about the available Bible resource. 

## Study Tools

Click **Study Tools (1)** to access additional resources that support your Bible study, including Commentary, Parallel Bible, ISLV, Audio, and More Tools.

<img src="/img/assets/version3/readbible5.png"  width="400px" alt="readbible5" className="img-border"/>

Once you click the **Study Tools** icon, the **resources (2)** available to support your Bible study are displayed at the bottom of the screen. These include **Commentary, Parallel Bible, Audio, ISLV**, and other study tools.
